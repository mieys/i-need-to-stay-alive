## About This Game (EN)

```
[h2]Survive the night. Together.[/h2]
Potpourri is a pixel-art co-op survival action game. You start in a cozy little house with your grandmother - until the ground begins to shake. Creatures pour out of the dark, tier after tier, each wave tougher than the last. Pick your hero, build your loadout and stay alive as long as you can, alone or with up to 8 friends.

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]14 unique heroes[/b] - every hero has a passive, three active abilities (Q / E / R) and evolution cards that change how they play.
[*][b]20 weapons that fight beside you[/b] - weapons follow your hero and attack on their own. Upgrade them through tiers and unlock special traits and effects.
[*][b]Build as you level[/b] - pick level-up cards, visit the travelling merchant and put together powerful items from recipes.
[*][b]Co-op for up to 8 players[/b] - online rooms (optionally password-protected) or LAN. Heal, shield and revive your friends and send them gold.
[*][b]Escalating tiers and giant bosses[/b] - from slimes and skeletons to minotaurs, wendigos and banshees. Then keep going in Endless Mode.
[*][b]Defend your home[/b] - survive the opening siege, day and night cycles, rain and storms. Hide in bushes, complete world quests and escort convoys.
[*][b]Pets and permanent progression[/b] - earn Soul Shards on every run and spend them in the Soul Shop on new heroes, weapons, pets and permanent upgrades.
[*][b]Several maps[/b] - forests, a dark swamp, golden dunes and underground labyrinths.
[*][b]10 languages[/b] - English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Turkish.
[/list]
```

## About This Game (TR)

[h2]Geceyi atlat. Birlikte.[/h2]

Yaratıklar karanlıktan akın ediyor, kademe kademe, her dalga bir öncekinden sert. Kahramanını seç, donanımını kur ve tek başına ya da 8 arkadaşınla birlikte olabildiğince uzun süre hayatta kal.

[h2]Öne Çıkanlar[/h2]

[*][b]Yanında savaşan 20 silah[/b] - silahlar kahramanı takip eder ve kendiliğinden saldırır. Kademelerle geliştir, özel özelliklerini ve efektlerini aç.
